# A461 public archive publication boundaries

- 1,796 A461 round files are unmodified copies of the originals, including M19/M21 derived-direction tensor files.
- 184 round files are published after redacting private machine references. Both original and public SHA-256 digests are recorded in `MIGRATION_MANIFEST.json`.
- 24 DOCX/PDF manuscript drafts, not primary experiment protocols, remain withheld pending publication review. Exact paths are listed in `MIGRATION_MANIFEST.json`.
- 127 research documents from outside the experiment folders are mirrored under `data/A461/context`, with hashes in `CONTEXT_MANIFEST.json`.

The G2 and M1-M22 activation-capture releases have not yet been verified as public. M23 hidden-state captures remain server-side. These are tracked separately.

Private originals have not been removed. Third-party models and unlicensed data are not redistributed.
